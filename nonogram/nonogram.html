Nonogram

Three-Strikes Mode
About

Nonogram is logic puzzle in which you're presented with a grid of cells that have an unknown on/off state. Numbers above and to the left of the grid indicate the lengths of segments that are active.

This version of the game is written and designed by Jacob Ewing

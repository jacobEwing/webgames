How to play

Guess the hidden five-letter word. Type a word and press Enter.

Each guess is scored by how many of its letters appear in the hidden word, counting duplicates correctly. For example:

  • If the word is poops and you guess poppy, the score is 3 — the two ps and one o match.
  • If the word is poppy and you guess poops, the score is still 3.

A score of 5 does not necessarily mean you've won — the letters may be in the wrong order. You win only by guessing the exact word.

Use the letter tiles at the top to keep track. Click a letter to cycle it through three states:

  • Plain — no information yet.
  • Red — you've ruled this letter out.
  • Green — this letter is in the word.

Reveal gives up and shows the answer. New game starts a fresh round.

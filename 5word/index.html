How to play

Guess the hidden five-letter word by typing a word and pressing Enter. You can take as many guesses as you like

Each guess is scored by how many of its letters appear in the hidden word - duplicates are only counted if they exist in both.

A score of 5 does not necessarily mean you've won — the letters may be in the wrong order. You win only by guessing the exact word.

You case the letter tiles at the top to keep track. Clicking a letter will cause it to cycle through three different states:

  • Plain — no information yet.
  • Red — you've ruled this letter out.
  • Green — this letter is in the word.
